Questions people actually ask

Is this a subscription?

No. One payment, kept forever. There is nothing to cancel.

What happens on a different device?

Premium unlocks on the device you redeem it on, because ReviseGo has no accounts server — your progress lives in this browser. Keep your unlock code and you can redeem it on another device too.

Do I lose my progress if I clear my browser data?

Yes — everything is stored in this browser. Use Export save on your profile page first; it downloads a file you can import back afterwards.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Reply to your Stripe receipt within 14 days and it will be refunded, no questions. Premium stays unlocked on this device until you clear it.

Is my card safe?

ReviseGo never sees it. Payment happens on Stripe's own checkout page, and this app has no card form anywhere in it.
